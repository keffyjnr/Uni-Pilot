About the Department

Forestry and Wildlife Management

The Department of Forestry and Wild Life Management is one of the academic departments in the Faculty of Agriculture at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko. The department is part of the University's Bachelor of Forestry Programme and contributes to the training of graduates in forestry and wildlife management.
