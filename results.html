Check Your Results

A simple guide to accessing and understanding your academic results.

1

Go to the AAUA AVERS Portal

Visit the official AAUA AVERS portal to access your academic information and results.

Open AAUA AVERS Portal →
2

Log In to Your Account

Enter your matriculation number and password to log in.

Login Details: Use your matric number and your surname as your password.

3

View Your Results

After logging in, navigate to your results section to view your academic performance.

Your GPA is displayed clearly at the top of your results.

⏳

When Are Results Released?

Results are usually released around one to two months after examinations.

📋

Missing a Result?

If a course result is missing, contact your Class Representative or Level Adviser for assistance.

Having Trouble Logging In?

If you are unable to log in to the AAUA AVERS Portal, visit ICTAC for assistance.
