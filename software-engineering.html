19.2 · Introduction & General Background

Software Engineering

It is with great pleasure and enthusiasm that I welcome you all to the Department of Software Engineering. Whether you are a new student embarking on your academic journey, a returning scholar, or a respected faculty member, we are honored to have you as part of our dynamic and innovative community.

Software Engineering is at the heart of modern technological advancements, driving solutions across industries such as finance, healthcare, education, and artificial intelligence. In this department, we are committed to equipping our students with the technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, and hands-on experience needed to thrive in an ever-evolving digital world.

Our distinguished faculty, cutting-edge research initiatives, and strong industry collaborations provide a solid foundation for learning, creativity, and professional growth. We encourage students to embrace innovation, engage in collaborative projects, and develop solutions that address real-world challenges.

As we embark on another academic session, I urge you to take full advantage of the resources available, participate in workshops and hackathons, and contribute to groundbreaking research in Software Engineering. The future belongs to those who are willing to learn, adapt, and create.

Once again, welcome to the Department of Software Engineering. Together, let us code, innovate, and build a smarter tomorrow!

Thank you.
Dr Mrs E.O Aliyu

The software development industry presents huge opportunities within the context of an expanding global economy that is increasingly becoming digital. With the enormous potentials of this sector of the economy and the ever increasing need for large and complex software systems, there is great promise to grow a large crop of software engineers as a force for sustainable socio-economic development. In addition to its core Computer Science foundation, Software Engineering also involves human and technical processes, and therefore borrows and adapts from the field of project management as well as from traditional engineering practice.

Programme Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Philosophy

The philosophy of Software Engineering focuses on producing graduates who have the required knowledge and skills to develop and maintain quality software systems of scale for governments, organisations and businesses that adequately fulfil the functional and nonfunctional requirements of the systems within time and budget constraints.

Objectives

  1. Provide a solid foundation in computing, including problem solving, algorithm design, data structures and programming basics.
  2. Demonstrate practical skills in requirements analysis, system design, software architecture, software metrics, verification and validation, and the software engineering process in general for the production of high quality software-based systems.
  3. Demonstrate expertise in programming in different languages, with emphasis on robust, reliable, cost-effective and secure systems based on sound design and development principles.
  4. Train students to effectively and efficiently manage the development of large, complex and critical software.
  5. Enable students to acquire the requisite knowledge and skills, practical exposure and high ethical standards for professional career opportunities, including self-employment, in the software industry.

Programme Duration

UTME: 4 years minimum. Direct Entry: 3 years minimum.

Admission Requirements

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

  1. Five O'Level credit passes at SSCE (WAEC, GCE, NECO and NABTEB) in not more than two sittings, including English Language, Mathematics and Physics, plus two from Chemistry, Biology/Agric Science, Statistics, Additional Mathematics, Computer Studies, ICT or Basic Electronics.
  2. UTME subjects must include Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, and one of Biology, Chemistry, Agric Science, Economics, Geography or Computer Studies.
  3. Candidates successful in the pre-degree programme (Science) of Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko can also be considered.

Direct Entry Requirements

Students entering 200 level must satisfy one of the following, provided the O'Level requirements above are met:

  1. Passes in two A'Level subjects, which must include Mathematics.
  2. At least Upper Credit in ND Computer Science, Statistics or Electronics/Electrical Engineering.
  3. NCE with at least Merit in Computer Science, Mathematics or Statistics.
Graduation Requirements

Graduation Requirements

  1. Pass all core courses, university and faculty/school-required courses and electives.
  2. Accumulate a minimum of 153 course units for students admitted through UTME and 121 course units for students admitted to 200 level, as stated in the prospectus.
  3. Attain a minimum CGPA of 1.00.

To graduate, a student must be found worthy in character throughout the period of study and accumulate the total units prescribed for the programme from Core, Faculty, General Studies courses, SIWES, Seminar and Final Year project.

Source note: The graduation paragraph in the supplied prospectus mistakenly names Data Science. Course Registration

Registration for Courses

  1. Candidates must register for appropriate compulsory and elective courses as indicated in the prospectus.
  2. Compulsory courses: Courses students must offer and pass before graduation.
  3. Elective courses: Department-prescribed courses required to be registered and passed to meet the University's minimum requirements before graduation.
  4. The minimum workload is 15 units and the maximum is 28 units per semester.
  5. A student may withdraw from a course within the Senate-stipulated period without penalty only with HOD approval and completion of an Add and Delete form. Withdrawal without permission is deemed a failed course.
  6. Any course examination sat for without registration will be cancelled.
