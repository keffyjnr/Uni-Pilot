About the Faculty

Welcome to the Faculty of Computing at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, a community of innovators, thinkers, and problem-solvers preparing students for the opportunities and challenges of the digital age.


The Faculty is committed to providing a strong learning environment in computing and related digital disciplines. Its academic areas include software development, artificial intelligence, data science, information and communication technology, information systems, and cybersecurity.


Students are encouraged to develop practical skills, critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, and problem-solving abilities while learning from academic staff and researchers.


Explore the departments below to discover the programmes and academic information available within the Faculty of Computing.

Programmes

B.Sc. Computer Science Study computing principles, programming, algorithms, software, systems, and related areas of computer science.
B.Sc. Cyber Security Focus on protecting computer systems, networks, applications, data, and digital environments.
B.Sc. Data Science Explore data analysis, computational methods, statistics, and techniques for extracting useful insights from data.
B.Sc. Information and Communication Technology Study digital technologies, communication systems, information processing, and ICT applications.
B.Sc. Information Systems Explore the design and use of information systems that support organisations, people, and business processes.
B.Sc. Software Engineering Focus on software development, software design, testing, maintenance, and engineering practices.
