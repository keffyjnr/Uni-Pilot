About the Department

Information and Communication Technology

The Information and Communication Technology programme is part of the Faculty of Computing at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko. The programme focuses on the use of computing and communication technologies to support information systems, digital services, technology-driven solutions and modern computing needs.
