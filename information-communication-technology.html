Introduction & General Background

Information and Communication Technology

It is with great pleasure and immense honor that I welcome you all to the Department of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko.

In the fast-evolving digital era, ICT plays a fundamental role in shaping the way we communicate, work, and interact with the world. Our department is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and industry collaboration, ensuring that our students are equipped with the knowledge and skills needed for the ICT field.

We take pride in our dynamic curriculum, state-of-the-art facilities, and dedicated faculty who are passionate about driving technological advancement. We foster an environment of creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving to prepare future leaders, innovators, and professionals in the ICT field.

To our new students, welcome to a journey of learning and discovery. You are now part of a community that values knowledge, collaboration, and continuous growth. We encourage you to take advantage of the resources available, engage with your peers and mentors, and embrace challenges as opportunities for growth.

As we embark on this academic programme, let us remain steadfast in our pursuit of knowledge, innovation, and excellence. The future of ICT is bright, and with dedication and hard work, we can drive meaningful change and contribute to the ever-evolving digital landscape.

Dr. A. O. Akingbesote
HOD-ICT

General Overview: Globally, there is a growing demand for Information and Communication Technology specialists to power and drive processes in business and non-business organisations. Information and Communication Technology has tremendous potential to boost operations efficiency, increase productivity and operate uninterruptedly with no downtime. The degree programme aims at producing ICT graduates with sufficient professional knowledge and skills to manage organisations' computing infrastructure and provide adequate support for computer users in order to enhance productivity and increase business value.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Philosophy

The guiding principles of the programme is to equip Information and Communication Technology students with adequate skills and knowledge to provide seamless organisations' ICT integration for effective productivity and to support organisations' computer system users through best practices and professional standards.

Objectives

  1. Provide students with a broad knowledge and practical skills in Information and Communication Technology.
  2. Empower students to be problem-solvers through Information and Communication Technologies tools.
  3. Equip students with the capacity for professional practice in the Information and Communication Technology sector.

Admission

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

  1. Five O'Level credit passes at SSCE (WAEC, GCE, NECO and NABTEB) at not more than two sittings, including English Language and Mathematics, Physics, and two from Chemistry, Biology, Agricultural Science, Statistics, Additional Mathematics, Computer Studies, Studies, ICT or Basic Electronics.
  2. UTME subjects must include Mathematics, Physics, and one of Biology, Chemistry, Agric Science, Economics, Geography or Computer Studies.
  3. Candidates successful in the pre-degree programme (Science) of Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko can also be considered.

Direct Entry Requirements

Students coming into 200 level must satisfy one of the following:

  1. Passes in two A'Level subjects which must include Mathematics, provided the O'Level requirement is satisfied.
  2. At least Upper Credit Pass in ND Computer Science, Statistics or Electronics/Electrical Engineering, provided the O'Level requirement is satisfied.
  3. NCE with at least Merit Pass in Computer Science, Mathematics or Statistics can also be considered, provided the O'Level requirement is satisfied.
Graduation

Graduation Requirements

  1. Passed all core courses, university and faculty/school required courses and electives.
  2. Accumulated a minimum of 146 course units for students admitted through UTME and 113 course units for students admitted to 200 level.
  3. Attain a minimum CGPA of 1.00.
  4. Be found worthy in character throughout the period of studentship and accumulate the total units prescribed for the programme from Core, Faculty and General Studies courses as well as SIWES, Seminar and Final Year Project.
Registration

Registration for Courses

Course Categories

Compulsory Courses: Courses which students must offer and pass before graduation.

Elective Courses: Departmental-prescribed courses required to be registered and passed to meet the minimum requirements of the University before graduation.

Registration Rules

  • The minimum work load is 15 units and the maximum is 28 units per semester.
  • A student can withdraw from a course within the time stipulated by Senate without penalty only with the approval of the HOD on completion of Add and Delete form.
  • Any student who withdraws without due permission will be deemed to have failed the course.
  • Any course examination sat for without registration will be cancelled.
