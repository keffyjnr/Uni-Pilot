WELCOME & BACKGROUND

About the Department

Based on the 2025/2026 Faculty Brochure.

Welcome to Biochemistry at AAUA. The department's welcome message emphasises academic excellence, innovation and intellectual curiosity. The B.Sc. programme provides broad-based knowledge of chemical processes in living organisms, from single-celled to multicellular plants and animals.

Core areas: Clinical Biochemistry, Food and Nutritional Biochemistry, Environmental Toxicology, Nutritional Toxicology, Molecular Biology, Bioinformatics and Drug Discovery, and Biotechnology.

Programme philosophy: Build broad biochemical knowledge, practical laboratory skills, scientific curiosity and an appreciation of biochemistry's contribution to life sciences and wider development.

LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT

Programme Objectives & Skills

  • Provide broad and balanced biochemical knowledge and practical skills.
  • Apply knowledge to theoretical and practical problems in biochemistry.
  • Develop transferable skills for biochemical and non-biochemical employment.
  • Prepare students for further study in specialised and multidisciplinary areas.
  • Show the value of interdisciplinary approaches to complex life problems.
  • Appreciate biochemistry's role in industrial, economic, environmental, technological and social development.
Unique programme features
  • Develops cognitive abilities in biochemistry and related sciences.
  • Introduces tropical flora and fauna and potential uses in drug development.
  • Builds laboratory and instrumentation skills.
  • Develops scientific information literacy and independent learning.
  • Strengthens critical thinking for industrial, environmental and life-science problems.
Employability skills
  • Critical thinking, problem-solving, analysis and interpretation.
  • Creativity, innovation and personal expression.
  • Research, scientific questioning and inquiry.
  • Oral and written communication.
  • ICT literacy and data analysis.
  • Understanding of industrial biochemical processes.
  • Entrepreneurship and small/medium enterprise development.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

Admission Requirements

Confirm current requirements with AAUA before applying.

UTME · 100 Level

Five SSCE/SSC or equivalent credit passes at not more than two sittings, including English Language, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry and Physics, plus an acceptable UTME result.

Direct Entry · 200 Level

The brochure says candidates with five SSCE (or equivalent) credit passes and at least two relevant advanced-level passes in Biology and Chemistry through GCE Advanced Level, IJMB or JUPEB may be considered. Confirm current rules with AAUA Admissions.

DEGREE COMPLETION

Graduation Requirements

UTME candidates

Expected duration: 4 years. The brochure states a minimum of 135 units.

Direct Entry students

Expected duration: 3 years. The brochure states a minimum of 106 units, including compulsory courses such as GST 111 and GST 112.

The brochure states that students must take and pass at least 8 units of elective courses by programme completion. Confirm current rules for your entry route and cohort.
