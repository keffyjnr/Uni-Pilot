Introduction & General Background

Animal Science

Welcome to the Department of Animal Science at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria. The Department provides broad-based, functional and practical training in Animal Science, with emphasis on scientific training, teaching, research, extension and entrepreneurial skills development.

The programme is designed to respond to the needs of the immediate environment and agricultural sustainability by developing appropriate manpower to improve animal production. It prepares students with theoretical knowledge and practical skills to contribute to animal production, research, entrepreneurship, agriculture and national development.

General Overview: Animal Science is the study of animals that live alongside humans and the science and business of producing domestic livestock species, including beef and dairy cattle, horses, poultry, sheep, goats, swine and rabbits. It also covers companion animals, including their nutrition, care and welfare. The discipline draws on animal behaviour and management, genetics, nutrition, physiology, reproduction and meat science. A broad knowledge base prepares graduates for careers across animal agriculture and related fields.

The programme combines formal scientific training with practical understanding of animal production, care and use. It addresses the biology and management of livestock and companion animals, and the application of scientific knowledge to productive and sustainable animal agriculture.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Philosophy

The philosophy that guides the training of students in Animal Science is to adopt practical and realistic approaches to produce skilled manpower furnished with comprehensive knowledge of animal biology and livestock genetic resources, together with the technologies required to improve animal yield and productivity.

The programme seeks to produce graduates with technical, productive and entrepreneurial skills for animal agriculture, livestock farm management and related enterprises, while developing the research inclination needed for careers in academic fields.

Objectives

  1. Produce graduates knowledgeable in animal breeding and genetics, physiology, behaviour and handling, nutrition and biochemistry, husbandry practices, animal health care management, and animal products and by-product technology.
  2. Equip graduates with the skills to establish and profitably operate animal and livestock enterprises.
  3. Contribute to the production of animal-based raw materials for industrial development.
  4. Develop technical and entrepreneurial skills to apply scientific knowledge to practical solutions and advance the animal/livestock subsector.
  5. Develop the capacity to process livestock into useful products and participate across livestock value chains.
  6. Prepare graduates to create demand-driven technologies responsive to local needs and ecological challenges for sustainable animal agriculture and livestock productivity.
  7. Build knowledge of animal-waste management and wealth creation from animal-waste resources.
Unique Features & Employability

Learning, Practical Training & Career Skills

Unique Features of the Programme

  • Addresses knowledge gaps arising from technological innovations in animal agriculture.
  • Provides knowledge for the care and management of work and companion animals in line with global trends.
  • Prepares graduates for present and future animal-production challenges through opportunity identification and innovative solutions.
  • Encourages entrepreneurship and the development of graduates who can become self-employed or employers.
  • Includes practical training on animal farms and related enterprises in the fourth year.

Employability Skills

  • Independent work and research with minimal supervision.
  • Verbal and written communication, including reports, publications and presentations.
  • Active listening, interpersonal skills and teamwork.
  • Time management and meeting deadlines.
  • Problem-solving and sound decision-making.
  • Organisation, planning and task coordination.
  • Ability to learn, adapt and respond to change.
  • Self-motivation, resilience and pride in achievement.

21st-Century Skills

Curiosity, critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, teamwork, digital literacy, information literacy, communication, media literacy, flexibility, leadership, initiative and social skills.

Duration

UTME candidates spend a minimum of four academic sessions (four years). Direct Entry candidates admitted into 200 Level spend a minimum of three academic sessions (three years).

Admission

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

  1. Obtain the required UTME score and meet the University's admission conditions.
  2. Have five Senior Secondary Certificate (SSC) credit passes, including English Language, Mathematics, Biology or Agricultural Science, Chemistry and Physics.
  3. The five required credits must be obtained in not more than two sittings.

Direct Entry Requirements

  1. At least two A-Level passes or equivalent in Chemistry and Biology, together with the five SSC credit passes prescribed for UTME candidates.
  2. Diploma holders with at least Lower Credit and the required five SSC credits, obtained in not more than two sittings, may be considered for admission into 200 Level.
Graduation

Graduation Requirements

Students must complete the minimum course-unit requirements for their entry route, pass all compulsory and required courses, and fulfil the programme's seminar and research requirements.

  • Minimum of 125 course units for the four-year UTME programme.
  • Minimum of 103 course units for the three-year Direct Entry programme.
  • Attend periodic seminars and submit the required written report for assessment at the end of the year.
  • Pass all compulsory courses and the required electives.
  • Complete a supervised undergraduate research project and submit the project report. This develops problem-solving, research, literature-review and report-writing skills.
Registration

Registration for Courses

Course Categories

GST Courses: General Studies courses required as part of the programme.

ENT Courses: Entrepreneurship courses included in the programme.

Compulsory Courses: Courses that students must register for and pass.

Elective Courses: Approved options from which students select the required number of units.

Course Registration Guidance

Students should register for courses according to their level, semester, entry route and the approved programme structure. The course tables below distinguish compulsory (C) and elective (E) courses and show lecture hours (LH) and practical hours (PH).

Students should confirm elective choices and registration details with the Department before completing registration.
