Welcome & Background

About the Department

Welcome to Industrial Chemistry at AAUA.
Chemistry explores the composition, structure and properties of matter, the changes matter undergoes, and the energy involved in those changes. This page focuses on the B.Sc. Industrial Chemistry programme and presents the course structure supplied in the 2025/2026 Faculty Brochure.

Department background: The Department of Chemical Sciences, formerly known as the Department of Chemistry and Industrial Chemistry, was among the pioneer departments in the Faculty of Science when the university relocated from Ado-Ekiti to its present site at Akungba-Akoko. The department offers B.Sc. Chemistry and B.Sc. Industrial Chemistry. Uni Pilot presents these as separate guides to make each programme easier to find.

Study areas: Organic, inorganic, analytical and physical chemistry, spectroscopy, environmental and green chemistry, natural products, polymers, materials, and applied chemical research.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

This is a student-friendly summary of the Chemistry programme's study areas, not a quotation of an official philosophy statement.

Programme Philosophy

To build a sound foundation in chemical principles and scientific reasoning, supported by practical laboratory skills, analysis, research and an understanding of how chemistry applies to materials, the environment and everyday life.

Learning Objectives

  1. Develop a sound understanding of fundamental chemical principles.
  2. Build competence in laboratory practice, observation, measurement and chemical analysis.
  3. Understand chemical structures, reactions, kinetics and mechanisms.
  4. Apply chemistry to environmental, material, industrial and other practical problems.
  5. Develop research, data interpretation, communication and problem-solving skills.
  6. Prepare students for further study and relevant scientific careers.

Normal Duration

UTME entry: 4 years (8 semesters). Direct Entry: 3 years (6 semesters), subject to the applicable university regulations.

🔬

Core Study Areas

Industrial processes, process science, petroleum and polymer chemistry, unit operations, catalysis, macromolecules, agrochemicals and chemical technology.

🧪

Practical Learning

Laboratory practicals, industrial attachments, seminar and research project work listed in the course structure.

Admission

Admission Requirements

Requirements transcribed from the supplied 2025/2026 Faculty Brochure. Applicants should confirm the current requirements with AAUA and JAMB.

UTME Entry Requirements

  • Five O’Level credit passes in not more than two sittings.
  • Required subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, and Biology or Agricultural Science.
  • Normal programme duration: four years.

Direct Entry Requirements

  • A’Level passes in Chemistry and Physics or Mathematics; or
  • OND/HND Upper Credit in a relevant field, as stated in the supplied brochure.
  • Normal programme duration: three years.

Maximum Duration

The supplied brochure states a maximum of two additional sessions beyond the normal duration: up to 10 semesters for UTME entry and up to 8 semesters for Direct Entry.
