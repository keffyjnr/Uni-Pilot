About the Faculty

Welcome to the Faculty of Science at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, a vibrant academic community where scientific excellence meets societal relevance.


The Faculty is committed to rigorous scientific education, impactful research, innovation, and the development of students who can contribute meaningfully to society. Through its diverse programmes, practical learning, and research activities, students are prepared for careers and lifelong contributions to science and humanity.


We welcome newly admitted and returning students to pursue academic excellence with diligence, integrity, critical thinking, and a strong sense of responsibility. The Faculty also welcomes alumni, industry partners, government agencies, NGOs, and other stakeholders to support research, mentorship, innovation, and opportunities for students.


The Faculty of Science is a community of learning, research, and innovation. Explore its departments, discover its programmes, and connect with the opportunities available within the Faculty.

Programmes

B.Sc. Animal and Environmental Biology Study of animals, their environments, biodiversity, and ecological systems.
B.Sc. Biochemistry Study of the chemical processes and molecular systems of living organisms.
B.Sc. Chemistry Study of matter, its properties, reactions, composition, and applications.
B.Sc. Industrial Chemistry Application of chemistry to industrial processes, products, and production.
B.Sc. Geology Study of the Earth, its materials, history, processes, and resources.
B.Sc. Applied Geophysics Application of geophysical methods to investigate the Earth's subsurface and resources.
B.Sc. Mathematics Study of mathematical theories, methods, reasoning, and applications.
B.Sc. Industrial Mathematics Application of mathematical methods to scientific, industrial, and real-world problems.
B.Sc. Microbiology Study of microorganisms and their roles in health, industry, environment, and research.
B.Sc. Physics and Electronics Study of physical principles alongside electronics and related applications.
B.Sc. Plant Science and Biotechnology Study of plants, their biology, improvement, and biotechnological applications.
