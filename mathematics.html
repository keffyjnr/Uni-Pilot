WELCOME & BACKGROUND

About the Department

Programme overview, philosophy and academic purpose from the 2025/2026 Faculty Brochure.

Programme overview: The B.Sc. Mathematics programme equips students with a solid foundation in mathematical theory, analytical reasoning and problem-solving. It builds confidence and independence in addressing diverse challenges, helping graduates apply mathematical knowledge in academic, professional and real-world settings.

Philosophy: The programme aims to develop academic excellence and competence in mathematical reasoning and problem-solving through logic and computational skills. It is also intended to help meet national needs in technological advancement, a continuing global priority.

Programme context: Mathematics and Industrial Mathematics are offered within the Department of Mathematical Sciences. Uni Pilot keeps their guides separate so students can quickly find the correct programme information.

LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT

Programme Objectives & Employability Skills

Programme Objectives

  • Build enthusiasm for mathematics and appreciation of its applications in different areas.
  • Provide a broad, balanced foundation in mathematical knowledge and practical skills in statistics and computer science.
  • Develop the ability to apply mathematical knowledge to theoretical and practical problems.
  • Build transferable skills useful in mathematics-related and other employment.
  • Prepare students for advanced study in specialised or multidisciplinary areas involving mathematics.
  • Develop appreciation of mathematics in industrial, economic, environmental and social contexts.

Employability Skills

  • Learning and innovation skills.
  • Life and career skills.
  • Information, media and technology skills.
  • Quantitative reasoning.
  • Ability to manipulate precise and intricate ideas.
  • Numeracy and logical problem-solving.

ENTRY & GRADUATION

Admission Requirements

Requirements reproduced from the supplied 2025/2026 Faculty Brochure.

Indirect Entry · 100 Level

At least credit-level passes in five subjects at SSC/O’Level or equivalent, including English Language, Mathematics and Physics, plus credits in any two of Chemistry, Biology or Agricultural Science. An acceptable UTME result with the appropriate subject combination is also required.

Direct Entry · 200 Level

Two relevant GCE/IJMB Advanced Level passes (grades A–E) in subjects such as Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics or Chemistry; or a good diploma with at least Upper Credit in Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science and Engineering, as stated in the brochure.

Graduation Requirements

The brochure states a minimum of 120 credits for UTME students and 90 credits for Direct Entry students. Confirm the requirement that applies to your cohort with the department.

PROGRAMME REQUIREMENTS

Credit Units & Industrial Experience

Minimum Credits for Graduation

The brochure specifies a minimum of 120 credits for UTME entry and 90 credits for Direct Entry students. These requirements are reproduced as supplied; check with the department if a newer regulation applies to your cohort.

Industrial Attachment & Research

Industrial Attachment I (AAUA-MTH 200) is scheduled during the long vacation of 200 Level, and Industrial Attachment II (MTH 300) during the long vacation of 300 Level. The 400 Level second semester includes MTH 400 Project.
