Introduction & General Background

Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

I am delighted to welcome you to the Department of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence, Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria.

The Department of Data Science evolved to become a unique academic unit in the University. It is supported by dedicated academic and non-academic staff committed to high standards of teaching, mentoring and research.

Our programmes expose students to basic and applied courses in Computing and Data Science, as well as Industrial Training, to prepare graduates for manpower needs in the public and industrial sectors.

The department trains competent Artificial Intelligence and Data Science experts and related professionals for national manpower needs and technological development.

Dr. Oluwafemi Oriola
HOD – Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

General Overview

Organisations accumulate large quantities of data for market research, disaster prediction, investment analysis, manufacturing, sales, customer behaviour, security intelligence, policy development, artificial intelligence and healthcare. Data scientists use analytical and technical skills to harvest intelligence from data and support decision-making.

Programme competencies include computational and statistical thinking, mathematical foundations, model building and assessment, data curation, knowledge transference, analytics and information management.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Philosophy

To equip Data Science students with the foundations, methods and implications of data science, extracting knowledge from data using techniques and theories from Mathematics, Statistics, Computing and Information Technology. The programme provides broad knowledge of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence for academic and industry pathways.

Objectives

  1. Develop skills to analyse computationally intensive real-life problems and independently build data-driven solutions.
  2. Build competence in the four A's of data: architecture, acquisition, analysis and archiving.
  3. Train students to discover information in data across business, government, healthcare, education, data engineering, big data and analytics.
  4. Prepare students to integrate data-driven IT solutions and business processes to meet organisational information needs effectively.

Admission

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

  1. Five O'Level credits at SSCE (WAEC, GCE, NECO or NABTEB), in no more than two sittings, including English Language, Mathematics, Physics and at least one of Chemistry, Biology/Agricultural Science, Statistics, Additional Mathematics, Economics, Geography, Computer Studies, ICT or Basic Electronics.
  2. UTME subjects: Use of English, Mathematics, Physics and one of Biology, Chemistry, Agricultural Science, Economics, Geography or Computer Studies.
  3. Successful candidates from the AAUA pre-degree programme (Science) may also be considered.

Direct Entry Requirements

Applicants entering 200 Level must satisfy one of the following, subject to the O'Level requirement:

  1. Passes in two A'Level subjects, including Mathematics.
  2. At least Upper Credit in ND Computer Science, Statistics or Electronics/Electrical Engineering.
  3. NCE with at least Merit in Computer Science, Mathematics or Statistics.

The prospectus admission subsection is headed “B.Sc Degree in Computer Science”; the programme title, surrounding text and stated award identify this as the B.Sc. Data Science programme. The page therefore uses the consistent programme title.

Graduation

Graduation Requirements

  1. Pass all core courses, University and Faculty/School required courses, and electives.
  2. Accumulate at least 145 course units for UTME admission or 115 course units for Direct Entry admission, as stated in this section of the prospectus.
  3. Attain a minimum CGPA of 1.00.
  4. Be found worthy in character and complete prescribed Core, Faculty, General Studies, SIWES, Seminar and Final Year Project requirements.

The programme summary elsewhere states 147 units for the four-year programme. This conflicts with the 145-unit graduation requirement.

Registration

Registration for Courses

Course Categories

Compulsory: Courses students must offer and pass before graduation.

Elective: Department-prescribed courses required to meet University graduation requirements.

Registration Rules

  • Minimum workload: 15 units per semester.
  • Maximum workload: 28 units per semester.
  • Withdrawal within the Senate-approved period requires HOD approval and completion of an Add and Delete form.
  • Withdrawal without due permission is treated as failure.
  • An examination taken for an unregistered course will be cancelled.

Industrial Training

Students’ Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES)

Programme placement

The prospectus lists DTS 298 – SIWES I in 200 Level, Second Semester. It also states that SIWES (INS 398) is held during the long vacation of 300 Level.

300 Level assessment courses

The 300 Level, Second Semester course structure lists five compulsory assessment components: report writing, industrial-based assessment, site-visit assessment, logbook assessment, and seminar presentation/examination. Together, these listed courses total 15 units.

Code note: The prospectus uses DTS 298 for the 200 Level SIWES I course, refers to INS 398 in the long-vacation note, and labels the 300 Level assessment courses CSC-SIWES 398A–E. These identifiers have been retained as printed pending departmental confirmation.
