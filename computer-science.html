Introduction & General Background

Computer Science

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria.

The supplied department handbook describes the Department of Computer Science as an academic unit established to produce highly trained graduates in Computer Science and Information Technology. It notes the department's commitment to teaching, mentoring, research and technological development.

The B.Sc. Computer Science programme covers basic and applied computing and information technology, including the algorithmic process and computational machines, hardware and software systems, computer programming, systems analysis and software development.

The programme aims to prepare graduates who can understand the impact and applications of computing and contribute to public and industrial sectors.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Philosophy

The philosophy of the Computer Science programme is to provide broad, high-quality education that emphasises the theoretical and algorithmic foundations of computing, guiding the design, implementation and application of computational systems.

The programme is designed to give students broad Computer Science knowledge and the opportunity to tailor their studies to their interests and needs across academic and industrial fields.

Objectives

  1. Build students' awareness of and enthusiasm for Computer Science and its capabilities.
  2. Provide a broad and balanced foundation of Computer Science knowledge and practical skills.
  3. Develop the ability to formulate real-world computing problems and use appropriate tools and technologies to find valid solutions.
  4. Develop students' ability to analyse, evaluate and propose alternative software or algorithm designs.
  5. Encourage self-management, teamwork, professional and ethical practice, effective communication and lifelong learning.

Duration

Candidates admitted through UTME normally spend a minimum of 4 years, while Direct Entry candidates normally spend a minimum of 3 years.

Admission

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

  1. Five O'Level credit passes at SSCE (WAEC, GCE, NECO or NABTEB), in not more than two sittings, including English Language, Mathematics and Physics, plus two from Chemistry, Biology/Agricultural Science, Statistics, Additional Mathematics, Computer Studies, ICT or Basic Electronics.
  2. UTME subjects must include Mathematics, Physics and one of Biology, Chemistry, Agricultural Science, Economics, Geography or Computer Studies.
  3. Candidates successful in the Science pre-degree programme of Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko may also be considered.

Direct Entry Requirements

Applicants seeking admission into 200 level must satisfy the O'Level requirements and one of the following:

  1. Passes in two A'Level subjects, including Mathematics.
  2. At least an Upper Credit in ND Computer Science, Statistics or Electronics/Electrical Engineering.
  3. NCE with at least a Merit in Computer Science, Mathematics or Statistics.
Graduation

Graduation Requirements

  1. Pass all core courses and required university and faculty/school courses.
  2. Accumulate at least 140 course units for UTME entry or 108 course units for Direct Entry students admitted at 200 level, as stated in the supplied handbook.
  3. Attain a minimum CGPA of 1.00.
  4. Be found worthy in character and complete the prescribed Core, Faculty, General Studies, SIWES, Seminar and Final Year Project requirements.
Registration

Registration for Courses

Course Categories

Compulsory Courses: Courses which students must offer and pass before graduation.

Elective Courses: Departmental-prescribed courses required to be registered and passed to meet the minimum requirements of the University before graduation.

Registration Rules

  • The minimum work load is 15 units and the maximum is 28 units per semester.
  • A student can withdraw from a course within the time stipulated by Senate without penalty only with the approval of the HOD on completion of Add and Delete form.
  • Any student who withdraws without due permission will be deemed to have failed the course.
  • Any course examination sat for without registration will be cancelled.
