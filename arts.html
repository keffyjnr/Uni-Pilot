About the Faculty

You are welcome to the Faculty of Arts of Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria.

The Faculty is in effect a Faculty of Humanities. Its programmes are designed to develop vibrant, thinking and creative minds who can become agents of change and contribute meaningfully to national development.

The Faculty also engages in research adapted to its peculiar situation and environment, with the aim of enriching global research across its individual areas and disciplines.

The Faculty currently states that it houses seven departments offering ten degree programmes altogether. The verified full list of degree programmes and the seventh department's name are coming soon.
