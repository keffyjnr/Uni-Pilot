Welcome & Background

About the Department

Welcome from the Head of Department
Welcome to the Department of Animal and Environmental Biology at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko. The programme explores animal life and the relationships between organisms and their environment, combining classroom learning with laboratory and field-based study.

General overview: The department was established as one of the university's foundation departments in 1999. The university's programme description highlights environmental processes, biodiversity, conservation, pollution control, sustainable food production and aquatic living resources. The department's history notes that it was renamed from Environmental Biology and Fisheries to Animal and Environmental Biology in 2015.

Areas of specialization include: Fish Biology and Production, Genetics and Molecular Biology, Parasitology, Ecology, and Environmental Studies.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Philosophy

To provide a sound understanding of traditional and modern zoology concepts, meet societal needs, and equip students with practical skills relevant to animal biology and environmental study.

Objectives

  1. Develop understanding of animal biology, ecosystems and biological organisms.
  2. Build subject-specific and transferable skills for fisheries, disease control, laboratories, teaching and forensic-related work.
  3. Provide a foundation for postgraduate study and professional careers.
  4. Enable students to apply scientific knowledge to practical problems and to design, conduct and interpret research.
  5. Build interest in zoology across levels of biological organization and in modern molecular techniques.
  6. Strengthen communication, presentation and lifelong-learning skills.
  7. Develop critical thinking, computational and numerical skills.

Admission

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

  • Five relevant O'Level credit passes, including English Language, Mathematics, Biology and Chemistry, plus one other relevant science subject (preferably Physics).
  • UTME subject combination supplied: English Language (compulsory), Biology, Chemistry, and Physics or Mathematics.
  • An acceptable UTME result is required for 100-Level entry.

Confirm current-year eligibility and subject combinations with the official AAUA/JAMB admission requirements before applying.

Direct Entry

The supplied programme document states five relevant SSC-equivalent credit passes, with two at Advanced Level. The following additional qualifications were also supplied and should be checked against the current AAUA admission notice:

  • ND/NID: minimum Upper Credit
  • HND: minimum Lower Credit
  • NCE: minimum 8 points
  • JUPEB: minimum 6 points
  • First degree: Second Class Lower (2:2) or higher
  • A-Levels: passes in two relevant subjects, subject to programme rules

Applicants must complete JAMB Direct Entry registration and AAUA screening when the respective portals open, and submit the required results and certificates.

Assessment & Graduation

Examination, Grading and Degree Classification

Examination System

  • Each course is examined at the end of the semester in which it is offered.
  • Continuous assessment may include tests, practicals, written assignments and other accepted methods.
  • Each course is scored out of 100%; continuous assessment contributes 30%.
