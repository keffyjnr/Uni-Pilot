About the Department

Fisheries and Aquaculture

The Department of Fisheries and Aquaculture is part of the Faculty of Agriculture at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria. The programme prepares students with the scientific knowledge, technical competence and practical skills required across capture fisheries and aquaculture.

Fisheries and aquaculture contribute to food and nutritional security, employment, livelihoods and foreign-exchange earnings. The programme also considers the role of aquatic resources in sustainable development and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Its training is designed to prepare graduates for entrepreneurship, employment, research and further studies, while responding to national needs and the changing global fisheries and aquaculture industry.

Programme Philosophy, Objectives & Skills

Programme Direction

Philosophy

The programme provides general and in-depth knowledge in different areas of Fisheries and Aquaculture, with emphasis on applying the discipline to food and nutrition security, employment and livelihoods, and sustainable ecological and socio-economic development. Students are trained in qualitative and quantitative approaches through a multidisciplinary perspective.

Objectives

  • Produce proficient graduates with broad fisheries knowledge to meet national high-level manpower needs, with an orientation towards entrepreneurship and self-employment.
  • Provide theoretical and technical knowledge across fisheries and aquaculture, and the ability to apply analytical tools to societal and economic problems.
  • Equip graduates with current techniques for sustainable fish production and careers in River Basin Authorities, government ministries, industrial fish firms, fish farms and development agencies.
  • Prepare graduates for postgraduate studies in specialist areas of Fisheries and Aquaculture.

Unique Features

  • Training in both capture fisheries and fish culture (aquaculture).
  • Coverage of the environmental, economic and social pillars of sustainability.
  • Courses spanning upstream and downstream activities across the fisheries and aquaculture value chain.
  • Newer subject areas include the Blue Economy and Entrepreneurship in Fisheries and Aquaculture.

Employability Skills

  • Critical thinking and problem-solving.
  • Communication and presentation.
  • Creativity, innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Teamwork, collaboration and interpersonal communication.
  • Self-management, planning, organisation and task management.
  • Leadership, accountability and social responsibility.

21st-Century Skills

  • Communication
  • Critical thinking
  • Problem-solving
  • Collaboration

Direct Entry

Eligible diploma holders may be considered for admission into 200 Level, subject to the stated entry requirements.

Admission

Admission Requirements

UTME Entry Requirements

In addition to an acceptable UTME score, candidates must possess five SSCE credit passes, including English Language, Mathematics, Biology or Agricultural Science, Chemistry and Physics, in not more than two sittings.

Direct Entry Requirements

Candidates must have at least two credit passes at Advanced Level or equivalent in Chemistry and Biology, in addition to five prescribed SSCE credit passes. Diploma holders in Fisheries and Aquaculture or a related field with at least Lower Credit, plus the required SSCE credits, may be considered for 200 Level.

Programme Duration

UTME: four years. Direct Entry: admission may be into 200 Level, subject to the university's applicable requirements.

Degree Award

B. Fisheries and Aquaculture.

Graduation

Graduation Requirements

Students must complete and pass the required courses and satisfy the university's other graduation conditions.

  • UTME: 128 credit units, comprising 8 GST units, 4 ENT units and 116 compulsory units.
  • Direct Entry: a minimum of 102 credit units, comprising 8 GST units, 4 ENT units, 4 AGR units and 90 compulsory units.

The supplied programme document states these totals. Students should follow the current approved departmental course registration advice.

Semester Allocation

Course Status & Hours
