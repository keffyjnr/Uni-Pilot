STUDENT GUIDE

Course Registration

Follow these steps carefully to complete your course registration successfully.

1

Log in to the student portal

Log in to your student portal using your registered student credentials.

2

Navigate to the course registration section

Locate and open the course registration section of the student portal.

3

Select your academic session and semester

Choose the correct academic session and semester for your registration.

4

Select your courses

Select the courses required for your level and department.

5

Review your selected courses carefully

Check your selected courses carefully and make sure all required courses are included.

6

Submit the registration

After confirming that your course selection is correct, submit your registration.

7

Print or download your course registration slip

Print or download your course registration slip after successfully submitting your courses.

8

Submit your course registration slip to your HOD

Submit the printed course registration slip to your HOD for proper documentation and signing.
