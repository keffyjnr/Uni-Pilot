WELCOME & BACKGROUND

About the Department

Based on the 2025/2026 Faculty Brochure.

Welcome to Industrial Mathematics at AAUA. The Department of Mathematical Sciences offers Mathematics and Industrial Mathematics, with a commitment to excellence in teaching, research and applying mathematical knowledge to real-world challenges.

Programme overview: Industrial Mathematics applies mathematical modelling, analysis and computational tools to problems in industry, finance and economics. It bridges theory and practice, helping graduates design efficient systems, optimise processes and support innovation in manufacturing, energy, logistics and technology.

Programme philosophy: To prepare graduates for careers as mathematicians in business and industry, with strong technical ability to apply mathematical solutions to real-world problems.

Unique features: The programme focuses on applying mathematics to industrial challenges, including financial and stock-related problems, while taking advantage of current technological innovations.

PROGRAMME INFORMATION

Objectives & Skills

Skills and objectives described in the 2025/2026 brochure.

Programme Objectives

  • Prepare students for demand in existing and emerging industrial sectors.
  • Develop enthusiasm for mathematics through a stimulating learning experience.
  • Provide a broad foundation in undergraduate mathematics and practical industrial applications.
  • Build the ability to apply mathematical knowledge to theoretical and practical problems.
  • Develop transferable skills and specialised skills in applied mathematics and multidisciplinary areas.

Employability & 21st-Century Skills

Creative and critical thinking, problem-solving, analytical thinking, initiative, quantitative reasoning, logical thinking, communication, time management, teamwork, independence, numeracy, computer and digital literacy, and the ability to work with precise and intricate ideas.

ENTRY & GRADUATION

Admission Requirements

Requirements transcribed from the supplied 2025/2026 brochure; confirm eligibility with AAUA Admissions.

UTME Entry · 100 Level

Five credit passes at not more than two sittings in SSCE or an equivalent qualification, including English Language, Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics and Biology/Agricultural Science or Economics. Candidates must pass UTME and the university screening.

UTME subjects: English Language, Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry or Economics.

Direct Entry · 200 Level

Two relevant A-Level passes (graded A–E) through GCE/JUPEB in subjects such as Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry/Economics, or a good diploma with at least Upper Credit in Mathematics, Physics and Engineering.

Graduation Requirements

Pass all core courses, university/faculty/school required courses and electives. The brochure specifies a minimum of 120 course units for UTME students and 90 course units for Direct Entry students, alongside passing all required courses.

PROGRAMME REQUIREMENTS

Credit Units & Industrial Experience

Credit Unit Requirements

The brochure lists a minimum of 120 course units for UTME entrants and 90 for Direct Entry entrants for graduation, subject to all required courses being passed. Confirm the applicable curriculum with the department.

Industrial Attachment & Research

Industrial Attachment I is listed as a 12-week placement during the long vacation of 200 Level. Industrial Attachment II is listed for 12 weeks at 300 Level. The 400 Level second semester includes a six-unit project.
