About the Department

Agricultural Extension and Rural Development

The Department of Agricultural Extension and Rural Development is one of the six departments in the Faculty of Agriculture at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko. The Department was created in the 2013/2014 academic session.

The Department trains students in the theory and practice of agricultural extension and engages in rural development activities with a view to improving farmers' standard of living through effective and efficient information dissemination to farmers.

The Department aims to produce graduates who can assume leadership positions, create employment opportunities, particularly in Agriculture, and improve the wellbeing of rural dwellers.

Areas of specialization include rural community development, rural women and gender studies, training in extension, extension administration, communication in extension, evaluation in extension, adoption studies, programme planning, group dynamics and leadership studies, and indigenous knowledge systems.
